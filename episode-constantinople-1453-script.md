## Fact checklist (script-stage verification — all claims in dialogue)
- Siege April 6 – May 29, 1453; final assault at dawn May 29. ✔
- Golden Horn chain: real. ✔ · Ships overland: night of April 21–22, ~70 ships over the Galata hill on greased log rollers. ✔
- Giovanni Giustiniani Longo: Genoese, ~700 men, commanded the land-wall defense; wounded in the final assault, evacuated to the ships; his removal is recorded as the morale break (died of the wound weeks later — post-episode, not claimed). ✔
- Orban's bombard: stone balls ~1,200 lb; ~7 shots/day ≈ "one shot every three hours". ✔
- "No army has ever broken these land walls": the Theodosian LAND walls were never taken by force before 1453 (the 1204 sack entered via the sea walls — pinned comment states it plainly). ✔
- Constantine XI: removed imperial insignia, died in the final fighting, body never reliably identified. ✔
- Angel legend at Hagia Sophia: recorded contemporary belief. ✔
- Mehmed II: age 21; the halted-looter anecdote (soldier breaking the pavement, Mehmed stopping him — the buildings were his) is from the near-contemporary accounts (Doukas tradition); dialogue renders it in plain words. Hagia Sophia converted immediately (first Friday prayers within days). ✔
- Hagia Sophia stands and is visitable today. ✔
- For the pinned comment (drafted at production time): the sack and enslavement (handled with care), the 1204 nuance, Morea/Trebizond to 1460/61, Giustiniani's death, Istanbul officially renamed 1930.
