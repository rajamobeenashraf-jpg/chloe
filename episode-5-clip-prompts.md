Pre-generation self-check (creative-direction.md §6–§16) run against every clip before submission — notes below each.

**Duration adjustments from the script table** (word-count math against realistic speech pace, documented before generating — none of these cut or reworded the actual lines):
- Clip 1: 10s → 15s → **now split into `clip1a_vista` + `clip1b_doomclock`, 10s each** (see below — the single-15s version paced too fast, an owner-flagged defect fixed by splitting rather than trimming words).
- Clip 4: 8s → **10s**. §8 requires the chew/swallow pause before the rating line lands; 8s doesn't leave room for both the eating business and the 22-word line.
- Clip 6: 8s → **10s**. 33 words plus the bell-ring/Tank-head-bob beat needs more than 8s to avoid a rushed ~4+ words/sec read.
All other clips fit their assigned duration comfortably at a natural pace. Runtime after the clip 1 and clip 5 splits (below): **126.5s** (was ~111s) — still inside the 90–200s Shorts spec, and this kind of upward drift from an initial estimate is the same pattern already precedented on Ep1/Ep2 (§9).

**Clip 1 split (owner-directed reshoot, see production log):** the single 15s `clip1_vista_v6` paced its dialogue at up to ~5 words/sec against a ~3.4 wps baseline elsewhere in the episode — an owner-flagged defect. Fitting all 61 words at a natural pace needs ~18s, past PAI's 15.2s hard per-clip cap, so clip 1 is now **two clips, `clip1a_vista` (10s) and `clip1b_doomclock` (10s)**, replacing the original clip 1 below. Owner separately asked for a more cinematic/dramatic background moment here; after fire-breathing was ruled out (no dinosaur ever had that trait — not an accuracy nuance, a physical impossibility), owner picked **two giant sauropods clashing over territory** — a deliberate, knowing anachronism for spectacle (see the species note below).

**Clip 5 split (owner-directed reshoot, see production log):** the original 8s clip 5 (a casual sighting-gag beat) was replaced by owner direction with a fuller T-Rex-family beat — introduce the family with real facts, stage a silent proximity-terror beat as the adult passes close, and land the "worst hiding partner" line with real predation stakes for Tank. The combined content doesn't fit PAI's 15.2s hard per-clip cap, so it's now **two clips, `clip5a_rexfamily` (10s) and `clip5b_rexfear` (12s)**, replacing the original clip 5 below. Clip 8 was also reshot in the same round to fix the "pushing" mechanism (see its section below).

**Species-accuracy fix (caught after generating clip 1 v1):** the first version of clip 1's prompt said "herds of distant dinosaurs" with no species named, and the model defaulted to generic long-necked sauropod silhouettes — paleontologically wrong for Hell Creek (a Late-Cretaceous North American formation with no sauropods; its real dominant herd herbivore is Edmontosaurus, a duck-billed hadrosaur). This is exactly the class of anachronism the craft study (§2) flags as the #1 comment-driver risk. Fixed by naming Edmontosaurus explicitly, then later switched to adult Triceratops (see production log's dinosaur-presence round) for clip 1's main herd. Clip 5's T. rex is correct as written — it's Hell Creek's real signature predator. **`clip1a_vista`'s sauropods are a separate, later, owner-directed exception** to this same accuracy fix — see the split note above; the owner explicitly chose to reintroduce the exact anachronism that was removed here, for this one specific spectacle beat, with full awareness of the tradeoff.
