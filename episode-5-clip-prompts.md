**Duration adjustments from the script table** (word-count math against realistic speech pace, documented before generating — none of these cut or reworded the actual lines):

- Clip 6: 8s → **10s**. 33 words plus the bell-ring/Tank-head-bob beat needs more than 8s to avoid a rushed ~4+ words/sec read.
All other 7 clips fit their assigned duration comfortably at a natural pace and are unchanged. New total runtime ≈111s (was ~102s) — still inside the 90–200s Shorts spec, and this kind of upward drift from an initial estimate is the same pattern already precedented on Ep1/Ep2 (§9).

She speaks directly to the lens with confident, brisk, energetic cold-open cadence (rapid but every word landing clear and crisp): "Sixty-six million years BC. Hell Creek. Tuesday. This is the whole world right now — no cities, no roads, no smoke on any horizon. And in about four hours, a rock six miles wide hits Mexico, and today becomes the worst day in the history of life on Earth. Nobody here knows. Nobody here even knows what a Mexico is." Her expression starts energized/thesis-statement confident and lands on something quieter and heavier by the final line. AMBIENT SOUND: a steady insect drone and low distant herd bass rumble under her voice throughout, continuous, no music sting. ${NO-RIG} ${TAKE} ${MATTE_CLAUSE} ${AESTHETIC}`
