The story of chai green 
It was 1823, and Britain was buzzing with the news of the discovery of a new tea variety growing in the lush hills of Assam... 
...Tender young plants and first samples of this new tea were urgently dispatched by ship to England. A local plantsman went with the plants, to make sure they arrived in peak condition. The crew named him Chai Green… 
(Green from his love of plants, and chai for the brew he made onboard for himself and the crew)

Had a great experience, was looking forward to coming here since I follow them on instagram! Ordered the Halwa Puri, their Desi Breakfast and the Schezwan Fries. Fries were highlight for me and my husband but everything was good and up to standards, already planning a visit back. 
This place has thr best butter chicken I have ever tasted. I recommend this dish with their FluffyPuri's. Their Desi Breakfast is pretty good too. Get this with the Paratha option. 
This is by far the best chai place around. The desi chai is amazing. You can tell by the food they use good quality food. Also the paratha is freshly made unlike other places that use the frozen ones. I'd definitely recommend. 
Lovely Indian breakfast for a great price! Delicious spiced channa with the Puri. I found the Keema a little too rich, highly recommend the Karak Coffee. 
Was recommended by a uber driver. We came for a trip from Sheffield would definitely come back if we are in Birmingham. Halwa Puri was lovely and even was offered a complementary dish. 
I would 100% be back to this place, the staff had really good customer service too came around a few times to make sure everything was ok and if we had any problems.
