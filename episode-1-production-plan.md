## PART A — The Character: "JOSIE"

An original persona — deliberately NOT a Chloe or Nova lookalike (clones get destroyed in comments; recognisable originality is the moat). She matches their production quality level but occupies the open lane both left vacant: **the American time-traveller with survival grit**.

> **Continuity note (2026-08-19):** the channel's locked production face (see `CHARACTER_LOCK.md`) diverged from this "Josie" concept — it's a fashion-model persona, not the copper-auburn/freckled look described below. This section is kept as the original brainstorm; the character's actual name and final look are still pending against the locked master.

### Identity
- **Name:** Josie (alternates if you prefer: Wren, Hazel — all read American + slightly timeless)
- **Age/look:** late 20s American; copper-auburn wavy hair, hazel-green eyes, light freckles, small beauty mark near the left eyebrow. Chosen for *thumbnail recognition* — red hair + freckles is instantly identifiable at feed size and clearly distinct from Chloe (brunette Brit) and Nova (sci-fi glam).
- **Signature modern outfit (her "2026 base look"):** white tee, olive utility overshirt, jeans, brown leather boots. She changes into period dress in every episode — the costume-change beat is a retention ritual (proven on Chloe).
- **Personality:** warm, dry-humoured, practical, a little stubborn. Where Chloe is a *tourist* ("look at this!"), Josie is a *doer* ("let's see if I can actually survive this"). She tries the work, not just the food.
- **Voice:** conversational American English, self-deprecating, short sentences under pressure. Verbal habits to repeat until they're brand: "Okay. New plan." · "The past smells like a decision I regret." · she rates every era's food out of 10 · she keeps a leather **field journal** shown once per episode (her version of Chloe's shower ritual — an ending ritual you own).
- **Format role:** talk-to-lens narrator-guide (Chloe mode) BETWEEN cinematic third-person set-pieces where she's the protagonist in the frame (Nova mode).
- **Channel name options:** *Josie Out of Time* · *The Josie Chronicles* · *Josie Across America* (keep "history/time" searchable in the tagline, not necessarily the name).

### Consistency workflow (critical for AI production)
1. Lock ONE face from the character-sheet generations I'm producing now.
2. Use that image as the reference input for every future image/video generation (most tools: character/style reference role). In Higgsfield specifically, you can train a reusable **Soul character** from 5–20 renders of her for locked-in consistency — recommended once you've approved the face.
3. Keep a one-paragraph "identity string" (the face description in the prompt above) and paste it verbatim into every scene prompt — never paraphrase it, or the face drifts.
4. Costume changes: re-state face identity string + new outfit. Face first, outfit second.
