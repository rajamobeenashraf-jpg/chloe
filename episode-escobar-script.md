## THE SCRIPT

### 1 · THE SEIZURE — cold open at the party [THIRD-PERSON] 14s
FIRST FRAME, mid-chaos: a 1987 Nápoles pool party in full swing — dancers, drinks,
a live band, beautiful guests — and HAZEL already SEIZED in the foreground: two men
pin her arms behind her; a third has a rifle raised, muzzle low, never touching her.
The band stops mid-note. The crowd parts. At the far poolside table, PABLO looks up.
*[Pablo]*, calm, not rising, across the silence: "You walk into my house with a
camera." (0.4) "Who sent you?"
HAZEL — held, chin up, steady: "Nobody. I came a very long way to hear your story."
A long beat. His eyes read her over the pool.
*[Pablo]*: "Release her." (the grips open; she straightens her shirt) "Sit."
The band resumes behind them.
— CINEMA: ONE move — a slow lateral dolly along the pool edge from the seizure to
include Pablo's table deep frame-right; FOV ~55°; hard noon sun, water caustics on
faces. BLOCKING: Hazel left third foreground (arms pinned — contact points: their
hands on her arms, her sandals on wet tile), rifle man frame-left edge, Pablo far
right third at the table, crowd parted along a diagonal. EYELINES: her eyes find
PABLO immediately (never the rifle — her fearlessness is the hook); his locked on
her; the guards watch HIM for the order. §17: the party stays alive at the edges —
frozen guests still breathing, drinks still held, water still moving.
— AUDIO: band mid-song CUT to silence on frame 1's grab <needle-drop band stop>,
pool lap, ice in glasses, distant macaw; {dialogue}; the band resumes under "Sit."
NO BGM. Edit: banner overlay "Visiting Pablo Escobar in 1987 🇨🇴"; tense drone
under the silence.
— END STATE ⛓: Hazel released mid-frame, Pablo's open hand at the empty chair, the
party re-animating behind.

### 2 · THE ASIDE [V-MODE lens] 9s
Following him along the pool edge, past dancers, hushed to her lens:
HAZEL: "Forbes just named him one of the richest men on Earth. In six years, he'll
be dead on a rooftop. He doesn't know. I do."
— CINEMA: her handheld lens, chest-up, walking forward (§14), party bokeh streaming
behind; one lens flare off the pool. EYELINES: lens; one flick to his back on "he
doesn't know."
— AUDIO: party swells muffled behind her voice, footsteps on wet tile; {her line};
NO BGM.
— END STATE ⛓: she lowers the lens; torchlight ahead at the dark lakeshore.

### 3 · THE LAKESHORE — the flex [THIRD-PERSON] 10s
The party spills to the torch-lit lake edge; guests dare each other toward the black
water. The water DETONATES — a hippo surfaces in a blast of spray; guests scream and
scatter, drinks flying. Hazel jumps a full step. PABLO does not move at all.
*[Pablo]*, gesturing across the dark water, host-proud: "Four hippos. From Africa.
What I want, I bring."
HAZEL, straight to lens, deadpan, party chaos behind: "He bought hippos."
(Her-style REACTIVE aside — she reacts, never instructs the viewer; the hippo plant
is carried by the image itself, per her plant-and-payoff grammar. Part 2's "Your
four" pays it off unsignposted.)
— CINEMA: ONE move — camera pushes past scattering guests to settle on the
two-shot; torches, spray catching the light. Hippo in CONTINUOUS motion (blow,
ears, wake) §17. EYELINES: everyone's eyes snap to the water — except Pablo's,
which stay on his guests, amused; hers to the hippo, to his unmoved profile, then
to LENS.
— AUDIO: <deep water blast>, screams breaking into laughter, torch crackle;
{dialogue}; NO BGM. Edit: playful theme sting on the blast.
— END STATE ⛓: guests laughing nervously back from the waterline; Pablo already
walking toward a low warehouse, beckoning her.

### 4 · THE NUMBER [THIRD-PERSON + lens tags] 13s
The warehouse door swings open: raw cash to the ceiling, canyon-deep.
HAZEL: "How much do you actually make?"
*[Pablo]*, patting a stack once: "Four hundred and twenty million dollars."
HAZEL: "A year?"
(0.4) *[Pablo]*: "A week." ← edit-stage CRASH ZOOM + total music dropout
HAZEL, to lens, wrapping a loose brick with a tossed rubber band: "So much cash that
rats eat ten percent of it."
*[Pablo]*, from deep in the stacks, not turning, dry: "The rats are a business
expense."
— CINEMA: ONE move — slow push past her shoulder into the cash canyon; slatted
board-light, dust motes; stacks past both frame edges. BLOCKING: contact — his palm
on the money, her fingers on the band. EYELINES: hers to him for both questions;
his on her through "a week"; her lens tag down-up from the band; his line thrown
away with his back half-turned.
— AUDIO: paper shift, <sharp band snap>, <small skitter deep in the stacks>,
muffled party far off; {dialogue}; NO BGM. — LOCK: cash only, never product.
— END STATE ⛓: the two silhouetted at the far door, daylight flaring.

### 5a · THE QUESTION [THIRD-PERSON] 3s
Walking out into the light. HAZEL, simple: "Were you always rich?"
*[Pablo]* stops. Looks at her. Quietly: "No." — his gaze drifts past her.
— CINEMA: static medium two-shot; the stop IS the beat. EYELINES: his hold hers,
then unfocus past camera — the memory cut lands on his eyes.
— END STATE: his unfocused stare.

### 5b · MEMORY — THE PROMISE [HAZEL-IN-THE-MEMORY] 12s
HARD CUT sold by audio: rain on tin. A Medellín hillside at dusk, decades back —
brick lanes, cooking smoke. A thin BAREFOOT BOY with dark curls looks down at the
city lights. HAZEL stands in the memory, exactly as she is, watching him. Quiet, to
lens:
"He grew up poor, right here in Medellín. He told his mother: I am poor — but I will
never die poor. I promise." (fact-verified: his documented line)
*[Pablo]* (present-day voice only, quiet): "I kept my promise."
— CINEMA: wide dusk frame, the boy small against the city; ONE move — slow drift to
her lens turn. Memory grade per prefix. EYELINES: boy at the city, never at camera;
Hazel at the boy, then lens.
— AUDIO: rain on tin, far dogs, city hum; {her lines}; his line clean V.O.; NO BGM.
Edit: the episode's single warm piano figure.
— END STATE ⛓: hard cut back to his present-day eyes refocusing.

### 6 · THE BARRIO — the turn [THIRD-PERSON] 10s
Golden afternoon, the street he built; kids on the concrete pitch. ROSA takes
Hazel's hand in both of hers:
*[Rosa]*: "Don Pablo built our whole street. God bless him."
HAZEL, to Pablo, level: "Why give it away?"
*[Pablo]*, watching the kids, quieter than anywhere else: "Because I remember."
HAZEL, apart, quiet to lens — jokes gone for good: "The help is real. But he kills
journalists. Judges. Policemen."
— CINEMA: ONE move — slow lateral drift with the street's life; long gold, ball
shadows. §17 populated. EYELINES: Rosa→Hazel; Hazel→Rosa→PABLO for the question;
his eyes STAY ON THE KIDS through the answer; hers to lens, steady.
— AUDIO: kids, ball, faint radio; {dialogue}; NO BGM. Edit: warm theme through
Rosa, hard OUT under her lens turn.
— END STATE ⛓: Pablo rolling the ball back; her lens lowered.

### 7a · SILVER OR LEAD [THIRD-PERSON] 5s
Veranda, tighter, amber. HAZEL: "And the people who say no to you?"
*[Pablo]*, perfectly calm, almost gentle: "Everyone accepts something. Silver… or
lead."
— CINEMA: LOCKED frame — no movement while he says it. EYELINES: his eyes hold
hers, unblinking. — END STATE: her blink — the memory cuts on it.

### 7b · MEMORY — THE CONGRESS [HAZEL-IN-THE-MEMORY] 9s
HARD CUT on a gavel: 1983, the Congress floor — a minister at the podium, arm
extended, pointing across the chamber; Pablo rising, buttoning his jacket, walking
out unhurried through the storm of voices. HAZEL at the chamber wall, hushed, to
lens:
"He got himself elected to Congress. The justice minister stood up and told the
country the truth. Months later, that minister was murdered." (fact-verified)
— CINEMA: ONE move — slow lateral track along the wall, Hazel foreground profile,
the confrontation deep in frame. Warm archival light. EYELINES: chamber→Pablo;
Pablo→no one; Hazel→podium→lens. — LOCK: the murder is spoken, never shown.
— AUDIO: <sharp gavel>, rising murmur, chair scrape; {her lines}; NO BGM.
— END STATE ⛓: hard cut back to his unchanged calm at the veranda.

### 8 · THE FEAR [THIRD-PERSON] 15s
Interior, ceiling fan, first true shade.
HAZEL, direct: "February. Your partner Lehder — taken to an American prison. Are
you afraid?"
The warmth stops: half-smile gone, jaw set, thumb pressing the armrest, no blink.
*[Pablo]*: "I would rather have a grave in Colombia…" (0.5) "…than a jail cell in
the United States."
He looks to the window. She turns to lens, hushed:
"Next month the Supreme Court kills the extradition treaty. He wins. For six years."
— CINEMA: tightest coverage — her clean single; his answer a slow push-in to
close-up; fan-blade shadow ticking his face. EYELINES: hers on him; his hold
through both halves; window-turn → her lens aside, one glance at his profile.
— AUDIO: fan tick, far thunder; {dialogue}; NO BGM. Edit: near-silence.
— END STATE ⛓: his profile at the window.

### 9 · THE MONOLOGUE [THIRD-PERSON] 16s
Dusk through the windows.
HAZEL: "If people remember one thing about you, what should it be?"
*[Pablo]*, slow, stating, not defending: "That I came from nothing, and I bowed to
no one." (0.5) "They will call me a monster. My people will call me a saint. Either
way — they will say my name."
— CINEMA: ONE move — the episode's slowest push-in, medium to close-up, dusk gold
crossing his face, ending tight on the heavy-lidded eyes; the longest-held shot of
Part 1. EYELINES: on her for sentence one; past camera to the middle distance for
sentence two; back to her on "my name."
— AUDIO: crickets beginning; {dialogue}; NO BGM. Edit: the one string swell.
— NOTE: his self-image, not the episode's verdict — Part 2 answers it.
— END STATE ⛓: tight on his face, the half-smile returning.

### 10 · THE CLIFFHANGER [THIRD-PERSON] 16s
He walks her toward the gate, host to the end, the mounted plane black overhead.
HAZEL: "How does this end, Pablo?"
*[Pablo]*, small smile, certain: "It doesn't. Colombia and I have an arrangement."
(0.4 — studying her face:) "You ask questions like someone who already knows the
answers."
HAZEL, holding his look: "I do know them." (0.5) "I'm from the future, Pablo."
His face changes — the first time all day. A silence.
HAZEL: "Do you want to see how it ends?"
HOLD on his eyes. SMASH TO BLACK. Card: 【PART TWO】
— CINEMA: walking two-shot settling face-to-face under the gate plane silhouette;
camera stops when they stop; the HOLD is a static close-up on his eyes — the only
freeze of stillness in the episode. EYELINES: mutual throughout; his narrow on the
noticing line; hers never leave his for the reveal (NOT to lens — this reveal is
for HIM); the audience reads his face.
— AUDIO: crickets, a horse led past, the party's last music far off; {dialogue};
dead silence after "how it ends" — cut. NO BGM. Edit: all music out from her
reveal; one sub-drop on the smash to black.
— END STATE: his eyes, held — Part 2's first frame continues from this exact
composition.
