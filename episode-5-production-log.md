Owner's instruction, verbatim intent: **nothing renders as a static frozen plate instead of full animated motion, and framing does not stay static overall — permanent, all future episodes, no exceptions.** This is now a standing pre-generation self-check item alongside the existing §10 checklist:
- **Any background subject meant to be alive (a herd, crowd, animal, moving machinery, etc.) must be explicitly directed to show continuous, visible locomotion for the ENTIRE shot** — state it concretely (legs stepping, heads moving, individuals visibly repositioned by the shot's end vs. its start), not just "grazing" or "moving" as a passing adjective. - **The framing itself should carry natural movement by default** — walking, a body-weight shift, camera-arm sway from real motion — rather than a planted, camera-locked "talking head" pose, unless a beat specifically calls for stillness (e.g., the crouch-and-hide beat, the trembling-hands beat) where stillness IS the direction.
