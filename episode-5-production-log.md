## PERMANENT RULE — owner lock 2026-08-21, MERGED into creative-direction.md §17 (2026-08-21, direct owner-authorized push, see note below)
**Caught on clip 1's first cut**: the background herd rendered as a frozen static plate (same pose held frame-to-frame, no locomotion) despite the shot being a 15s continuous take, and the overall framing was too static — she stood planted in one spot delivering the whole monologue with no natural body motion. Owner's instruction, verbatim intent: **nothing renders as a static frozen plate instead of full animated motion, and framing does not stay static overall — permanent, all future episodes, no exceptions.** This is now a standing pre-generation self-check item alongside the existing §10 checklist:
- **Any background subject meant to be alive (a herd, crowd, animal, moving machinery, etc.) must be explicitly directed to show continuous, visible locomotion for the ENTIRE shot** — state it concretely (legs stepping, heads moving, individuals visibly repositioned by the shot's end vs. its start), not just "grazing" or "moving" as a passing adjective. A single vague motion word is not enough to stop the model from defaulting to a still plate — verified twice this episode (clip1 first cut, and clip7's two failed herd-anatomy attempts before the herd was removed from frame entirely) that under-specified background motion is a real, recurring failure mode, not a one-off.
- **The framing itself should carry natural movement by default** — walking, a body-weight shift, camera-arm sway from real motion — rather than a planted, camera-locked "talking head" pose, unless a beat specifically calls for stillness (e.g., the crouch-and-hide beat, the trembling-hands beat) where stillness IS the direction.
- Fix applied this episode: clip 1 regenerated (`clip1_vista_v3.mp4`) with explicit continuous-locomotion language for the herd and her walking naturally through the ferns for the full shot instead of standing rooted in place.
