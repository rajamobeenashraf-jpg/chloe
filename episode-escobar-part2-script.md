## THE SCRIPT

### 1 · THE ANSWER — arrival morph [THIRD-PERSON] 12s
FIRST FRAME = Part 1's final frame: his eyes, held. Then, quietly:
*[Pablo]*: "Show me."
Her hand takes his. They walk INTO the gate arch — and within ONE continuous
framing, the world morphs around them: same gate, same mounted plane overhead — now
a ticket booth, tour buses, families in shorts taking selfies. HACIENDA NÁPOLES IS
REALLY A THEME PARK TODAY; the fact is the hook. He stops. Turns a slow half-circle.
*[Pablo]*: "…Where are my guards?"
HAZEL: "Gone. Everything is. Come."
— CINEMA: the series' signature shot — ONE move: a slow push through the arch that
never cuts while 1987 becomes 2026 (production: first/last-frame anchored morph;
1987 side chains from Part 1 clip 10's end frame). FOV ~55°. BLOCKING: the two
centered through the arch; 2026 crowd flows around them like water around stones.
EYELINES: his sweep the changed world; hers stay on HIM — his face is the show now.
— AUDIO: 1987 crickets crossfade INTO 2026 — reggaeton from a tour bus, children,
ticket-scanner beeps <scanner beep>; {dialogue}; NO BGM. Edit: banner "Bringing
Pablo Escobar to 2026 🇨🇴"; the morph carries a low riser.
— END STATE ⛓: the two facing the park interior, a tour group crossing ahead.

### 2 · HIS OWN TOUR [THIRD-PERSON] 14s
Inside the park, the ruin of his mansion behind ropes. A TOUR GUIDE leads a group
past — and the real Pablo stands INSIDE the crowd, in his 1987 clothes, listening
to his own life.
*[Guide]*, mid-spiel, gesturing: "This is where he kept his zoo. He was killed in
nineteen ninety-three—"
*[Pablo]*, to Hazel, quiet, level: "He tells it wrong."
HAZEL: "He tells it the way it ended."
A tourist strolls past wearing HIS FACE on a t-shirt. Pablo watches him go.
*[Pablo]*, deadpan: "He is wearing my face."
HAZEL: "They sell you now."
(0.4) *[Pablo]*: "…How much?"
— CINEMA: ONE move — slow drift with the tour group, Pablo and Hazel holding still
inside the moving crowd (the world flows past the fixed point — him). EYELINES: the
group on the guide; PABLO on the guide, unblinking; the t-shirt beat: his eyes
track the tourist across frame; Hazel watching Pablo, half-smiling at "how much."
§17: full tourist life.
— AUDIO: the guide's projected voice, crowd shuffle, a camera shutter; {dialogue};
NO BGM. Edit: light comic underscore, out before clip 3.
— END STATE ⛓: the group moving on; Pablo still looking after the t-shirt.

### 3 · THE CABLE CAR — the lookalike gag [THIRD-PERSON] 12s
A Metrocable gondola climbing over Medellín — the comunas below painted with
murals, football pitches, solar panels; the city he bombed, alive and enormous.
HAZEL, at the glass: "This is Medellín now."
A TEEN across the gondola lowers his phone, staring:
*[Teen]*: "No way. Bro looks exactly like him. Photo?"
Pablo turns his head slowly and just STARES. The teen takes the selfie anyway.
HAZEL: "He's not a lookalike."
*[Teen]*, leaving at the station, delighted: "Sick costume, man."
Hazel to her lens, one flat look: "I'm not explaining."
— CINEMA: ONE move — none; the gondola's own glide IS the movement, city drifting
beyond the glass (the cinematic freebie: motion without camera motion). EYELINES:
Hazel to the glass → the teen; Pablo's slow head-turn stare (the comedy is the
stillness); teen to his phone screen; her flat look to LENS.
— AUDIO: cable hum, wind tick on the gondola, <phone camera click>; {dialogue};
NO BGM.
— END STATE ⛓: doors opening onto a busy station platform.

### 4 · THE SHOW [THIRD-PERSON] 10s

### 5 · THE HIPPOS — the series payoff [THIRD-PERSON] 12s
A wide brown river at golden hour. HIPPOS EVERYWHERE — wallowing, bellowing,
calves among them. The Part 1 plant pays off.
HAZEL, quiet: "Your four. Over a hundred now. Nobody could ever move them."
Pablo watches a long moment — pride and something colder crossing his face.
*[Pablo]*, quiet: "They survived me."
— CINEMA: ONE move — slow lateral along the bank, the two in silhouette against
the water, hippos in CONTINUOUS motion at every depth (§17 — the shot must read

river; on his line his eyes drop to the water's edge.
— AUDIO: hippo bellows layered near/far <deep grunts>, river, evening birds;
{dialogue}; NO BGM. Edit: the warm piano figure from Part 1's memory returns —
the only theme reprise of the series.
— END STATE ⛓: the river at their backs, dusk deepening.

### 6 · THE WALL [THIRD-PERSON] 14s
A memorial wall of victims, candles at its base. No tourists here. (No legible real

HAZEL, reading, quiet and plain: "He was a judge. She was a journalist. He was a

HAZEL: "Say something."

### 7 · THE ROOFTOP [THIRD-PERSON] 20s
*[Pablo]*, finally, not looking at her: "You said you would show me how it ends."
CUT: an ordinary narrow street. Ordinary houses. One ordinary tiled roof. Evening.
HAZEL: "Here. December second, nineteen ninety-three. One day after your birthday."
*[Pablo]*, after a long moment: "Can it be changed?"
HAZEL: "It already happened. I'm here so you see it."
He looks up at the roof tiles a long time. Then — almost at peace — the series'
full-circle callback to his Part 1 grave line:
*[Pablo]*: "A rooftop in Colombia. Not a jail cell in the United States." (0.5)
"Then I win."
Hazel holds his profile in frame. She says nothing. (The wall said it already.)
— CINEMA: her question-singles minimal; his final line the series' LAST slow
push-in, ending tight on his upturned face against the roofline; the roof itself
kept ordinary — no spectacle, no reenactment, nothing shown but tiles and sky.
EYELINES: his up at the roof throughout the final line; hers on him; NEVER a lens
aside here — the audience is left alone with it.
— AUDIO: street quiet, a dog far off, laundry moving on a line <soft cloth>;
{dialogue}; NO BGM. Edit: silence, then a single low note under "Then I win."
— END STATE ⛓: his upturned face, the darkening sky.

### 8 · THE RETURN — coda [THIRD-PERSON → V-MODE] 16s
The park gate at dusk; through the arch, faint, the warm light of 1987 — his
world, waiting. He stops at the threshold, turns to her once:
*[Pablo]*: "My people are waiting."
He steps through. The light takes him. The arch is just an arch again.
HAZEL, alone, to her lens — plain, quiet:
"He went back. He changed nothing. His war killed thousands of people. Everything
else is gone. The only thing still growing… is the hippos."
(0.5) Soft: "Hazel — out of time."
— CINEMA: ONE move — after he vanishes, a slow drift from the empty arch to her
lens frame; night rising, the mounted plane a black shape overhead — the series'
first and last image, rhymed. EYELINES: his last look at her, then forward through
the arch; hers following him until the light goes, then to LENS for the coda.
— AUDIO: the 1987 party audible faintly through the arch for one moment — then
gone; night insects; one far hippo grunt from the dark <distant low grunt>; {her
lines}; NO BGM. Edit: resolving strings, silence under the sign-off. Hold to black.
