## What changed from v1, and why

**1. Event-focus reinforced (the cross-video finding).** Watching Chloe's own catalogue showed: her highest-engagement video ever (Aztecs, 3,088 comments) is event-led with the named figure barely present; her weakest recent subformat is the sit-down "asked X the tough questions" interview. v1 already kept Alexander to one late clip, which was already the right call — v2 doesn't add him anywhere else. New clip 7 below is that hinge, built on a real, verified fact.

**2. Repeated-number stat-drop device added** (Aztecs: *"60,000 people... 60,000!"*) — v1 stated big numbers once and moved on; v2's revised clip 5 states one, then repeats the bare number alone, matching the exact device.

**3. Three fact corrections from a verification pass (WebSearch, this session):**
- **Battle-order communication** — v1 invented "shouted, man to man, a mile down the line." Real Macedonian command relied on trumpets/horn-signals and officers riding the line, organized through a taxis/lochos hierarchy — not a shouted human chain. Fixed in clips 5 and 8.
- **The baggage-camp breakthrough, more precisely** — confirmed via Arrian: it happened once Darius had already begun to flee (late in the battle, not right after the opening horns); Persian/Indian cavalry broke through a *gap* in the phalanx line specifically to reach the baggage animals; the baggage was guarded mostly by *unarmed* men; the raiders freed prisoners being held there; a Greek-mercenary reserve line then hit them from the rear and drove them off, colliding with Alexander's own relief force. v1's version was close but vaguer — v2's clip 9 uses the real mechanism.
- **Alexander visiting the wounded** — real and sourced (Arrian, via secondary summary): *"For the wounded he showed deep concern... he visited them all and examined the wounds."* v1's line ("See that every one of these men eats before I do") was my invention, not sourced. v2's clip 12 has him actually examining a bandage himself, closer to the documented behavior.
- **New verified fact used for the hinge beat**: Darius III's mother (Sisygambis), wife (Stateira I), and two daughters were captured at Issus in 333 BC and were still being held — respectfully, in Alexander's baggage train — at the time of Gaugamela, two years later. Confirmed across multiple sources (Wikipedia's *Family of Darius Before Alexander* and *Sisygambis* articles, Ancient Origins). This is a genuinely strong, true, on-topic shock fact that nothing in v1 used.
