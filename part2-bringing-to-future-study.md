## What the new batch taught (view-weighted)

1. **The single strongest hook in the whole genre is a past-thing-becomes-present
   TRANSFORMATION in seconds 0–3.** The two mummy-reawakening reels (16.4M and 14.8M
   views) are pure transformation: artifact → living person → she sits up → walks.
   Staging pattern: identical framing across the morph (continuity anchors: same hat,
   same pose), SFX-driven (rehydration/crack sounds, a GASP at the eye-open), a
   micro-climax by second 6, then a payoff walk. The statue-crack reel (117K but
   41.9x its channel median) confirms: crack → shatter → living reveal, ~6s rhythm
   per figure, particle debris hiding the AI seams. Cosmina's Churchill
   painting-step-out is her version of the same device.
2. **The legacy-eulogy is the emotional payload of a future-reveal conversation.**
   The Elizabeth-and-time-traveler dialogue (1.1M, 26.7x median): fear → skepticism →
   the reveal ("we arrived from centuries in the future") → the eulogy climax ("500
   years hence, every man, woman and child knows your name"). Telling a person their
   own legacy IS the format's catharsis.
3. **The what-if/intervention lever is emotionally nuclear but must be handled with
   ethics.** The "saving late celebrities" reel (1M, 147x median): three-beat
   vignettes (inevitable tragedy → disruption → intimate closure). For our project
   the INVERSION is the power: Hazel explicitly CANNOT and WILL NOT change anything —
   the same restraint Cosmina's Anne Boleyn shows ("Your daughter will be all right.
   That's all I can say.").
4. **The open lane finding:** almost nobody is doing the full conversational
   bring-to-the-present episode at Cosmina's quality. The space is transformation
   clips, POV montages, and deepfake gags. A two-part Escobar with a real Part 2 is
   nearly uncontested territory.
5. POV arrival grammar (1776 reel, 37s): the wake-up/arrival device + escalating
   public event + micro-reactions of bystanders — useful for Part 2's arrival beat.

## THE TWO-PART ESCOBAR DESIGN (proposed to owner)

### Part 1 — "Visiting Pablo Escobar in 1987" (revisions to script v3)
1. **Hook line change:** Hazel no longer reveals the future in clip 1 — "Nobody. I
   came a very long way to hear your story." The audience keeps the irony via her
   lens whispers; HE stays in the dark all episode.
2. **"His Africa" upgrade:** the walking zoo beat becomes the OPEN-TOP JEEP SAFARI —
   he drives her through his own animals; giraffes crossing; a hippo blocks the road;
   he waits, unbothered: "They own the road. I only pay for it." Hippo PLANT stays in
   Part 1; the payoff moves to Part 2.
3. **Ending = the cliffhanger at the farewell.** Pablo: "You ask questions like
   someone who already knows the answers." → Hazel, holding his look: "I do know
   them. I'm from the future, Pablo." → his face changes for the first time all day →
   Hazel: "Do you want to see how it ends?" → CUT TO BLACK. "PART TWO."
4. Clips 10/11a/11b (future-cast, the date, the hippos) MOVE OUT of Part 1 into
   Part 2, where he can SEE them instead of Hazel narrating them. Part 1 lands at
   ~1:55–2:00. Pablo's monologue (8) stays in Part 1.

### Part 2 — "Bringing Pablo Escobar to 2026" (new script, to be written on owner GO)
Beat design, each element sourced from a studied mechanic:
1. **ARRIVAL HOOK (transformation device, mechanic #1):** No recap. His hand takes
   hers at the 1987 gate → they step THROUGH the gate arch and within one continuous
   framing the world morphs: same gate, same mounted plane — now with a ticket
   booth, tour buses, families taking selfies. Hacienda Nápoles is REALLY a public
   theme park today — the fact IS the hook. Banner: "Bringing Pablo Escobar to 2026
   🇨🇴". Pablo: "…Where are my guards?" Hazel: "Gone. Everything is. Come."
2. **HIS HOUSE IS A TOURIST ATTRACTION (Caesar gift-shop beat, in HIS OWN home):**
   tourists stream through the ruin of his mansion; a visitor walks past wearing his
   face on a t-shirt. Pablo, deadpan: "He is wearing my face." Hazel: "They sell
   you now." Pablo: "…How much?" (the Caesar "I want one" register).
3. **THE HIPPOS PAYOFF (the series' planted payoff):** the lake — hippos
   everywhere, wallowing, bellowing. Hazel: "Your four. Over a hundred now. Nobody
   could ever move them." Pablo, quiet: "They survived me."
4. **THE CITY HE BOMBED (legacy-eulogy inversion, mechanic #2):** Medellín 2026 —
   cable cars over the comunas, murals, kids with phones; someone watching a TV
   drama about him (unbranded). Pablo: "That is not me." Hazel: "It's the version
   of you the whole world watches." His Part-1 monologue prediction ("they will say
   my name") came TRUE — and it's not glory.
5. **THE WALL OF NAMES (the Mary-I-grade dark beat; intervention-inversion,
   mechanic #3):** a memorial with victims' names. Hazel reads three names aloud,
   plainly. For the first time in two episodes, Pablo has nothing to say. No music.
6. **THE ROOFTOP (the answer to Part 1's cliffhanger question):** "You said you
   know how it ends. Show me." An ordinary tiled rooftop on an ordinary street.
   "Here. December second, nineteen ninety-three. One day after your birthday."
   Pablo asks the only question left: "Can it be changed?" Hazel: "It already
   happened. I'm not here to change it. I'm here so you see it." Then HIS closing
   monologue — the series' chilling full-circle callback to his Part 1 grave line:
   looking at the rooftop, almost at peace: "A rooftop in Colombia. Not a jail cell
   in the United States." (beat) "Then I win." — and Hazel's coda answers it with
   the cost, over the hippo lake at dusk, + "Hazel — out of time."
7. **THE RETURN:** he steps back through the gate to 1987 — carrying what he now
   knows, changing nothing — per the restraint ethic. (Or Part 2 ends ON the
   rooftop beat + coda; final choice at scripting.)

### Why the two-parter beats one long video
- Part 1's cliffhanger is a subscription/follow engine ("Part 2" comment bait).
- The future-cast facts become SCENES he reacts to instead of narration.
- The hippo plant paying off across episodes builds the series habit.
- The rooftop callback ("Then I win.") only works if Part 1's grave line aired first.
